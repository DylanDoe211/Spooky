New Biome, Boggy Shipyard:
-This is a brand new cross-mod ecotone that generates inbetween the cemetery and ocean if there is no other biome in the way
-This biome is generally more focused on ambience, building blocks, but still has a couple of new items and things to do
-Underground in the caves you can find pirate treasure chests which drop one of four new weapons and a funny new potion
-It has new fishing content including a new rare weapon, an angler quest fish, and biome crates
-Contains new ghostly critters like fish and other underwater life, some of them passive, some of them defensive, and some aggressive
-On the biomes surface, you can find gigantic conch shells which can be interacted with using an EMF reader to begin a fun new mini-boss encounter
 
A FEW THINGS TO NOTE ABOUT THE NEW BIOME
-It is not a regular biome, but a spirit reforged ecotone which means the following
-You MUST have spirit reforged enabled with spooky mod for the biome to generate, otherwise it will never spawn during worldgen
-It is NOT guaranteed to generate in your world as spirit reforged ecotones are intentionally not guaranteed, you may need to create a few worlds to find one
-The size of the biome varies HEAVILY, sometimes you may get a big one, and sometimes you may get a really tiny one
-All of the blocks in this biome are obtainable even without spirit reforged enabled, meaning you may build an artificial one through normal spooky mod gameplay
(You can obtain the blocks and tree saplings from shipyard crates, which can be created by throwing bone crates into shimmer)

Spooky Player Internal Change:
-For those who do not need spooky mods source code for any reason, this change will basically mean nothing to you
-For those who do, spooky mod has had a giant bloated ModPlayer class called SpookyPlayer where every single player related effect was shoved into for multiple years of the mods development
-As of this update, almost every single thing in SpookyPlayer (Regarding armor set bonuses, accessories, ect) have all been moved to their own ModPlayers in the individual code files corresponding to the item or thing that causes it
-This, going forward, will be much better for keeping individual effects more organized, and also so people (Like wiki editors) who browse the source code can have an easier time finding things related to player effects
-To clarify though, SpookyPlayer still exists and now just contains pet variables and random miscellaneous things that dont really fit in an individual file
